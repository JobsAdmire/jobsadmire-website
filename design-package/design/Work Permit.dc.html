Two legal routes to work in Turkey

Law No. 6735 gives foreigners two doors: the standard work permit for real employment, and the e-Muafiyet exemption for short, specific assignments. Choosing the wrong one is the most common — and most expensive — mistake.

Rules & regulations

What the Ministry checks before approving

Most rejections are not about missing documents — they are about employer criteria. These are the rules under Law No. 6735 and its implementing regulation, checked on every application. When you hire through us, we pre-check all of them before anything is filed.Most rejections are not about documents — they are about employer criteria. We pre-check all six before filing.

Working without a permit costs
· Administrative fine per unauthorised worker — over ₺100,000 in 2026, updated every year · Deportation and entry ban for the worker — at the employer's expense · Block on the employer's future work permit applications · Possible criminal liability for company directors
1
The 1:5 employment ratio
At least five Turkish citizens on SGK payroll for each foreign worker, per workplace. Monitored monthly from the 7th month of the first permit — so a new employer has time to build up.
2
Financial capacity
The company must show paid-in capital or gross sales above the Ministry's thresholds — proof that the business can genuinely carry a foreign employee.
3
Role-based salary thresholds
Each role has a minimum gross salary set as a multiple of the Turkish minimum wage — higher for specialists and managers. Updated every January; underpaying is an automatic rejection.
4
No tax or SGK debts
Overdue tax or social security premiums suspend the application. Ministry, SGK and Revenue Administration databases are linked — discrepancies are flagged instantly.
5
SGK registration from day one
Once the permit is approved, the worker must be registered with social security immediately and paid the declared salary through the books.
6
Sector-specific rules
Health roles need Ministry of Health pre-authorisation; tourism and advanced-technology investments enjoy exemptions from some employment criteria; public projects are exempt from financial criteria.

Who qualifies for the work permit exemption

Article 48 of the implementing regulation lists exact categories. If the assignment fits one, the foreigner applies online — no employer criteria, no 1:5 ratio.

For the standard permit, eligibility depends on the employer instead — see what the Ministry checks or take the 60-second check above.

How long will your case take?

It depends on one thing: where the worker is right now.

Worker is abroad

~8–10 weeks
Week 1Work visa application at the Turkish consulate in the worker's country
Weeks 2–6Ministry filing within 10 working days of the consular application, then ~30 days processing
Weeks 6–9Approval, fees, visa issuance and travel to Turkey
ArrivalSGK registration and first working day

Worker already in Turkey

~4–6 weeks
Week 1Direct domestic filing on the Ministry system — needs a valid residence permit of 6+ months
Weeks 1–5~30 days Ministry processing — no consulate stage, no travel wait
ApprovalFees paid, SGK registration and first working day

Fastest route — worth checking before sourcing abroad whether a suitable candidate is already in Turkey.

What a work permit costs

Three cost lines — two go to the state, one to us. All quoted in writing before we start; the worker never pays anything.

State fee 1

Work permit fee

Charged per permit year by the Ministry (çalışma izni harcı) — the amount is set every January.

State fee 2

Card fee

A small fixed fee for printing the permit card (değerli kağıt bedeli). If the worker is abroad, the consular visa fee is added.

Our side

Service fee — fixed

Pre-check, filing, follow-up and SGK registration — one fixed amount, quoted upfront in writing. If a permit is rejected, we re-file at our own cost.
