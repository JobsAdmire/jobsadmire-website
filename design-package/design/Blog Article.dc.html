Turkey's factories, hotels, farms and construction sites are all competing for the same shrinking pool of local labor. Hiring from overseas is the practical answer — but the work permit process stops many employers before they start. This guide walks through it, end to end.

Key takeaways
→The employer applies, not the worker — and salary below the ministry minimum is the #1 rejection reason.
→You need 5 Turkish employees per foreign hire, plus 100,000 TL capital — shortage sectors get exemptions.
→Realistic timeline: 4–8 weeks end to end, including ~30 days of ministry review.
→A licensed agency can file and track everything — you never touch a form.

Who can hire foreign workers in Turkey?{{ secs.overview.chev }}

Work permits are applied for by the employer, not the worker. The Ministry of Labor evaluates your company before it evaluates the candidate. Two criteria matter most:

5 : 1 rule
Five Turkish employees registered at the workplace for each foreign worker you hire.
Capital / turnover
Paid-in capital of at least 100,000 TL, or gross sales that meet the ministry's threshold.

Sectors with proven labor shortages — agriculture, manufacturing, tourism — benefit from exemptions and quotas that a licensed agency can apply on your behalf.

Documents you'll need{{ secs.documents.chev }}

✓Signed employment contract
✓Worker's passport copy
✓Diploma or skill certificate
✓Company tax & activity records
✓Current SGK service statement
✓Online application form

The application, step by step{{ secs.process.chev }}

1
Job offer & contract. The employer and candidate sign a contract that states role, salary and duration.
2
Online application. Filed through the Ministry of Labor's e-permit system; the worker applies at the Turkish consulate in their country in parallel.
3
Ministry review. Evaluation typically takes about 30 days; missing documents restart the clock.
4
Approval & fees. Permit and card fees are paid; the permit doubles as a residence permit.
5
Arrival & SGK. The worker enters Turkey with a work visa and must be registered with SGK within 30 days of starting.

Mistakes that get applications rejected{{ secs.mistakes.chev }}

⚠Salary below the ministry's minimum for the role — the most common rejection reason.
⚠Contract details that don't match the online application.
⚠Applying while the 5:1 employee ratio isn't met.
⚠Missing consulate appointment on the worker's side.
