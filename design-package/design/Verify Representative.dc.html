See a red flag? Stop — then tell us

These five signs mean the person is not acting for JobsAdmire, no matter what their card or letter says. Do not pay, do not hand over documents — report it and we take it from there.

Red flags — stop immediately
Asks for money in any form. Cash, transfer, “processing fee”, “visa deposit” — nobody at JobsAdmire ever collects money from a worker.
No badge, or a QR that opens another site. The QR must open a record on this page — nothing else counts.
Contacts you from a personal number or email. Official contact is only company numbers and @jobsadmire.com addresses.
Wants to keep your original passport or certificates. Copies are enough. Originals always stay with you.
Pressures you to sign or pay today. A real offer survives a phone call to our office. Urgency is the fraudster’s tool.
