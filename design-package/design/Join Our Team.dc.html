How overseas roles work

Three ways to work with us from your country

A representative in Uzbekistan with four active partners has a very different week from someone helping us run one trade test. So we do not force one contract on everybody.

Most common
Full-time representative
You are our country. You hold the partner relationships, run interviews, check documents and answer for the quality of everyone we take from your market.
•Monthly fixed pay plus placement bonus
•Full CRM access, own dashboard and targets
•Listed on our site as an official representative
Alongside your job
Part-time coordinator
You already run an agency, a training centre or an HR desk. You give us agreed hours a week — sourcing, document checks, candidate briefings — and keep your own work.
•Agreed hours, agreed monthly fee
•Limited CRM access for your own pipeline
•Can grow into a full-time country role
One job at a time
Project-based
A defined piece of work with a start and an end: run a trade test for 40 welders, organise a live recruitment day, prepare one batch of documents.
•Scope, timeline and fee written before you start
•Good first step if we have not worked together
•Repeat projects for people who deliver
Every engagement is on a written agreement — scope, pay and how it ends. If you already have a licensed agency and want a company-to-company relationship instead of a role, that is our partnership track.
