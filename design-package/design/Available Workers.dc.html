What "verified" means on this page

Every profile passes four checks before it appears here — that is why the shortlist moves fast.

1

Documents checked

Passport validity, qualifications and certificates verified before the profile goes live.

2

Skill-tested

Practical assessment by our partner institutes or our own team — not just a CV claim.

3

Video interviewed

Recorded interview covering attitude, language level and readiness to relocate.

4

Permit-ready file

Goes live

Paperwork prepared so the work permit can be filed the moment you approve.

A profile that fails any check never reaches this page — what you see is what we can place.
