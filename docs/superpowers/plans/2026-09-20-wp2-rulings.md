# WP2 — ruling log (W1–W23 planning rulings; execution rulings appended below)

Durable record of the decisions the controller made while planning and executing WP2 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-20-wp2a-foundation.md`, `…-wp2b-pages.md`). A ruling is a decision that changed or filled a gap in the approved spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-18-website-programme-design.md`) or the WP1 rulings (`2026-09-18-wp1-foundation-rulings.md`, R1–R60). Where a ruling applies a spec §10 auto-default the owner has not overridden, it says so; the owner may override any of them, and the code is always the current source of truth.

**Why these exist.** The WP2 discovery (13 page readers + a critic, 2026-09-20) found ~20 mismatches between the designed forms and the Operations form catalog, cross-page contradictions in the headline numbers, and Phase A content that does not exist yet (no signed metrics, no photos, no Turkish blog bodies, no counsel copy). Every one of them needed a decision before a page could be ported; W1–W23 are those decisions.
