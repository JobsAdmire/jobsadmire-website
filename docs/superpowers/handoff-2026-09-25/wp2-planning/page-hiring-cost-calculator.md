## traps
- This page has no real form: all four 'Get an exact written quote' CTAs are WhatsApp links; wiring the `calculator` door means ADDING name/email (required by the catalog) plus phone/company/country/message inputs the design never shows — a design delta to log alongside the thank-you navigation.
- The door's `calculator` catalog has no salary/tier/toggles/turkishStaff fields — everything beyond headcount/trade/durationMonths must be serialised into `estimateSummary` (≤2000 chars); unknown keys are silently dropped, so a field sent under its own name vanishes without an error.
- Rates are not in strings and not in the bundle contract: building the page on package strings alone leaves the engine without a RateConfig — the contract (`contract/website-bundle.v1.ts`) and the LOCAL bundle need a `rateConfig` (and a `calculatorRoles` collection) before the calculator can render, and D17 wants the dated badge driven by `effectiveFrom`.
- legalMin rounding: the design rounds the legal minimum UP to the next ₺500 for the slider/guide/pass-check but prints the unrounded 1.5× figure in the threshold note — tests against 'worked figures' will fail unless the rule is chosen explicitly.
- The 26 leading-₺ TR strings and the 8 parity exceptions on this page are already in `src/content/lint-baseline.json`; adding sys.* strings with new lira amounts will trip the lint unless written as `38.944 ₺` in TR.
- Empty-TR fragments (calc.041/154/157/367) and EN-empty fragments (st1b1/st2a1/st2b1) break if concatenated; use one ICU message per sentence with rich-text tags.
- Homepage teaser and this page disagree on the model (2× Specialist tier; support auto vs opt-in): a shared engine will change one of the two pages' numbers — surface it before implementing either.
- The 5:1 timing claim contradicts the Work Permit page's legal strings (7th month vs day of application) — do not publish both unchanged.
- Design hard-codes '₺205.008' and '₺12.700' with TR separators inside the EN markup — derive them (2 × fine, 10 × support) with formatTRY instead of copying.
- WhatsApp prefill text, pass-check summary and the print flow are EN-only in the design; the print CSS isolates #calculator via body-wide visibility:hidden — Tailwind `print:` variants must reproduce it or the Print button prints the whole page.
- JSON-LD is injected client-side in the design with `inLanguage: 'en'` hard-coded and FAQ data only available after render — port server-side with jsonld.ts and per-locale values.
- Desktop values in the .dc.html are authored at 1.333× (`html { zoom: 0.75 }` line 30) — normalise per D19 before pixel-harness comparisons; mobile (≤1100px) values are 1:1.
