## traps
- terms.json is a glossary (8 words), not the Terms page — do not treat it as legal-page content; the Terms/Privacy bodies come from origin/main-backup public/locales/{en,tr}/{privacy,terms}.json (and tr/terms.json is untranslated English).
- D22 explicitly excludes 'website login' and the 'third product tile' — the design's sign-in/reset forms, lockout, domain auto-detect, ChatAdmire handoff and its computed chatadmire.jobsadmire.com host must not be built; /portal-girisi is a link-out page (I15).
- The design's CRM links point at the dead https://crm.jobsadmire.com/ext-login; the real targets are settings.portal.host + loginPath/forgotPath (portal.jobsadmire.com/auth/login, /auth/forgot-password); there is no settings entry for operations.jobsadmire.com.
- Policy numbers in the copy (6-char password, 3 attempts, 30-min idle, 30-min reset) contradict the CRM (8 chars, 5 attempts/15 min, 1-h reset, OTP) — never publish them.
- storeLinks.ios is null: any 'iOS 15+' / App Store badge / QR-to-jobsadmire.com/app copy is an unsupported claim; gate on the Android link only.
- The in-page TR dictionary of CRM Login.dc.html is stale relative to strings/crmlogin.json — always read TR from the catalogue.
- Several visible strings are JS-composed and absent from the catalogue (hours line, 'Sign in to ', attempts-left, reset-sent halves, placeholders, short tile labels, store-search sentence) — new copy is sys.* only.
- The locale layout hard-wires SiteChrome(default); the 'minimal' variant exists but is unused and there is no chrome-less route group — a design-faithful chrome-less portal page needs a layout decision first.
- Footer.tsx does not render the Privacy/Terms links the design has on every page (home.218/home.219) — easy to miss because the legal line home.228 is there.
- Newsletter: the Ops WebsiteForm 'newsletter' is inactive (404) until counsel clears it; the website must treat that 404 as 'form off', and the confirm/unsubscribe pages must exist regardless because NEWSLETTER_PATHS in newsletter-token.ts prints their slugs into mail the moment it is enabled; the unsubscribe page must accept the RFC 8058 POST; never smoke-test these routes with a real subscriber token.
- Slug drift trap: routing.ts pathnames and Ops NEWSLETTER_PATHS are a verbatim mirror — a TR slug change on either side 404s every mail link.
- Thank-you conversion fires only for a key in FORM_KEYS (src/analytics/forms.ts); a server action redirecting with an unlisted key silently loses attribution (R55).
- noindex set: /portal-login, /thank-you, /newsletter/* (routes.ts NOINDEX_PATHNAMES) — legal pages are indexable and bundle.pages is empty, so metadata fallbacks must be provided.
- design-package/data/*.sample.json are dev-only and irrelevant to these routes; nothing here may import from design-package/** at runtime (ESLint R56).
