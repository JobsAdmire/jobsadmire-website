## traps
- hire.141 is a deliberately-empty TR fragment (render '' — never fall back to EN), but its neighbour scTitleC has NO id at all — the two problems look identical and need opposite fixes (leave hire.141 empty; add a sys.* id for ' belgeli işçiler').
- The `hire` catalog requires `name`; the hero quick form has no person field — wiring both forms to `hire` unchanged makes the quick form a guaranteed 400.
- Consent is NOT a form field: it is the top-level `consentVersion` in WebsiteFormSubmitDto; the design's checkbox must map there, and the quick form has no checkbox.
- Unknown fields are silently DROPPED by the door (reported in `dropped`), so sending `city`, `dial` or `consent` as fields loses them with a 200 — fold city into `message` deliberately.
- Retry on any non-2xx (deploy 502 window) but never vary the payload: dedupe is (formKey, requestHash, hourBucket) and a retry must land on the same row.
- Design sector/timeline option values are the translated labels — a TR visitor stores Turkish strings in Inquiry.sector unless stable keys are sent.
- '470+', '13+', '22+' are hard-coded in markup, not strings — easy to port as literals and then trip the D17 numbers-out-of-copy lint once metrics exist.
- countryCount is computed from the country list (13 with Sri Lanka) while source-map.js plots 12 — the map and the heading disagree unless one list drives both.
- hire.043 TR is a concatenated trio; porting it verbatim duplicates two labels in Turkish.
- Copy strings hire.201/203/215/216 describe the WhatsApp mock flow; D13 requires /tesekkurler navigation — do not 'fix' by inline success, and do not edit legal-flagged hire.201 without approval.
- Design hides the whole quick form ≤700px and the sticky bar ≤700px; the site's breakpoints are lg/xl (Tailwind, 0.75-normalised) — map 700px deliberately, do not scale breakpoints (D19).
- FAQ questions in the design are spans inside buttons, not headings; the Accordion primitive renders a heading per trigger (headingLevel) — set 3 under the h2.
- The arrow inside each industries row is a nested interactive element inside a clickable row — restructure (button row + separate CTA link), or axe fails the gate.
- Absolute www.jobsadmire.com links in copy (work-permit, hiring-cost-calculator, privacy) must become locale-aware Link hrefs or TR visitors leave the TR tree.
- Analytics: the design's generate_lead carries roles/sector/headcount — the allowlist drops them; do not extend ALLOWED_PARAMS with free text (D13).
- External assets in the design (d3/topojson/world-atlas CDNs, Google Play and Apple badge images) are not loadable — pre-render the map SVG and use local badge SVGs; settings.storeLinks.ios is null.
